ORDER SAFETY

Before any real automation, the backend must enforce:

  • Maximum daily loss and equity drawdown
  • Position sizing based on account currency and symbol contract size
  • Spread and slippage limits
  • Broker minimum/maximum volume rules
  • Server-side stop-loss and take-profit validation
  • Emergency stop and duplicate-order protection
No order can be submitted from this prototype.
